DAVID & GYPSY — WEDDING WEBSITE, VERSION TWO

This is the forest-green and gold version inspired by your Knot website.
The original ivory/burgundy version is preserved separately in ../gypsy-and-david.
Your selected video remains the large main visual on the Home page.

All wedding details live in dist/wedding-config.js.
Open that file in a text editor and replace the empty quotation marks with your
information. Keep the quotation marks and commas in place. Save and reload.

Unknown values should stay as "". The website will display thoughtful
coming-soon messages. No date, venue, schedule, or guest information is invented.

WHAT YOU CAN EDIT
  names             Both names and the initials in the header
  date              The date exactly as you want guests to read it
  location          City / region shown over your film
  venue             The venue name in the celebration section
  welcome / story   Welcome message and optional personal story
  ceremony          Time, venue, address, and Google Maps link
  reception         Time, venue, address, and Google Maps link
  travel            Directions and optional map link
  accommodation     Hotel recommendations and optional booking link
  dressCode         Outfit guidance
  gifts             Your gift message and optional registry link
  rsvp              RSVP form URL, deadline, and optional message
  contactEmail      Email address for guest questions
  thingsToDo        Local recommendations and an optional link
  questions         Optional question/answer entries for the Q + A page

PAGES
Navigation switches between Home, Our Story, Dress Code, Q + A, Travel,
Things to Do, Registry, and RSVP. The URL hash preserves the selected page,
so links such as index.html#travel and the browser Back button work.
Ceremony information appears on Home once you fill in the ceremony fields.

RSVP
The RSVP section says "opening soon" until you add rsvp.url. Once supplied,
it shows a working button that opens your form in a new tab. This website does
not collect, submit, or store guest responses itself. Use your chosen RSVP
service for that. Blank map, booking, registry, and contact links remain hidden.

YOUR FILM
Source: Material/Videos/Video_20261003_205451_122.mp4
The full 28.27-second clip is optimized as dist/assets/our-film.mp4.
It loops automatically, initially muted so browsers allow autoplay. The Sound
off/on control turns the original audio on or off. A small pause/play button
remains at the lower left of the film. It pauses for visitors
who request reduced motion; they can choose Play film. Browsers or devices
that block autoplay can also use that control. A still from the film provides
a fallback while loading or if playback is unavailable.

YOUR PHOTO
dist/assets/our-story.jpg is a web-sized copy of Material/IMG_8986.jpg.

LOCAL PREVIEW
Open dist/index.html in your browser, or run "npm start" from this folder and
visit http://127.0.0.1:4173. No npm install or build step is needed.

PUBLISHING
dist/ contains the complete static website, including the video and photo.
It can be hosted as-is. The Sites hosting manifest is .openai/hosting.json.
Local edits must be republished to update the hosted site.

FILES
dist/wedding-config.js   Your editable details
dist/index.html          Page sections and default placeholder copy
dist/styles.css          Typography, colours, layout, mobile styles
dist/app.js              Configuration, navigation, and playback behaviour
dist/assets/             Optimized media and favicon

The site uses local Parisienne and Cormorant Garamond fonts and local assets.
Font licenses are included in font-licenses/ and dist/assets/fonts/.
It needs no external font service,
JavaScript library, tracking script, or paid subscription to run locally.
